# Grief Cartographer

A quiet web space for reflective self-expression: slow, anonymous and ephemeral, with no likes, comments or follower counts.

**Live prototype:** https://griefcartographer.vercel.app

## What it is

Grief Cartographer is an independent HCI research probe. People respond to reflective prompts across five spaces (Fog, Isolation, Immersion, Exploration and Growth) by drawing or writing. Contributions are released anonymously into a collective gallery, and a person cannot return to their own earlier submissions. To test this concept, we have also conducted the 12-user study, but 2 students requested to not get included afterwards so only 10 participants we have taken into the account for study. use has to sing-in and answer the 10 pre-questionnaire to have the basic idea there understanding toward the exixtign technologies.
accounts being made only for the purpose of the study the release of drawings is anonymous.

It asks one question: what happens to personal expression when a platform removes metrics, recommendations and permanence?

## Study materials
the quetionnaire included below mentioned questiones.

1. What is your age group?
2. What is your area of profession / field of study?
3. Do you consider yourself a self-aware or well-reflected person?
4. Have you ever faced a personal setback or loss that you feel significantly impacted your life?
5. Do you feel that modern digital platforms allow you to express yourself authentically?
6. Do you feel that mainstream apps provide a safe environment for expressing personal vulnerabilities?
7. What do you think about the concept of likes and comments on modern platforms?
8. Do you think people would express their vulnerability if proper anonymity is given?
9. Do you feel safe writing your emotions down on physical paper compared to screens?
10. How would you rate modern tech overall when it comes to supporting the self-expression of human vulnerabilities?

Responses are not published. Participation was voluntary, and findings are reported anonymously.

## Design principles

- **Slow:** no feeds, no recommendations, no pressure to respond quickly.
- **Anonymous:** contributions are not tied to a name or profile.
- **Ephemeral:** people release a contribution and do not revisit it.
- **No social metrics:** no likes, comments or followers.

## Research context

I ran a small pilot study with 10 university students aged 18 to 21: a pre-questionnaire, a one-to-one session with the tool, and an interview. Interview notes were coded by thematic analysis. I am preparing a short paper from it.

This is a pilot: the sample is small, and I was both the builder and the interviewer. No participant data, drawings or interview notes are stored in this repository.
